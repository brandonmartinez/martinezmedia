Martinez Media, LLC

Ludington, Michigan · 2011–2023

The story

Hi, I’m Brandon. My wife Joy and I ran Martinez Media out of Ludington, Michigan. I’d been doing freelance web and design work since 2002, as a teenager; by 2011 the volume justified making it real, so we formalized it as an LLC that February.

For twelve years it was primarily photography — weddings, families, and high-school seniors around West Michigan — alongside web design, branding, and social-media consulting for local businesses.

We wound the LLC down in January 2023. The photography never really stopped; what ended was the business entity, not the practice.
